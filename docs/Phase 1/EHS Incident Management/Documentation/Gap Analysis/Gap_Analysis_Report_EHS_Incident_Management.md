The engagement is focused on integrating NDT’s existing SharePoint-based incident reporting process into the Intelex EHS Incident Management application. The primary driver is enterprise process harmonization following integration of business units, while preserving NDT requirements around escalation, witness statements, incident classification, corrective action governance, restricted access to sensitive data, and reporting continuity.

NDT currently uses a customized SharePoint incident module where HSE managers administer incidents, employees provide information outside the system, and actions are tracked in a disconnected SharePoint action list. The existing Intelex solution is more workflow-driven and purpose-built by incident type, with configured incident objects for Injury/Illness, Near Miss, Vehicle Incident, Environmental Incident, Property Damage Incident, Master Incident, and supporting objects such as Witness, Incident Risk Assessment, RIDDOR, OSHA, WCB Claims, and related lookup objects.

## 2. Current-State Solution Summary

The current Intelex EHS Incident Management configuration is a multi-object incident management application rather than a single generic incident table. The `.ipack` shows configured workflow-enabled objects for **Master Incident**, **Injury/Illness**, **Near Miss**, **Environmental Incident**, **Vehicle Incident**, and **Property Damage Incident**, with supporting objects including **Witness**, **Incident Risk Assessment**, **RIDDOR**, **OSHA**, **WCB Claims**, **Notice of Violations**, **Involved Vehicle**, **Materials**, **Initial Treatment**, and various regulatory and classification lookup objects.

Current Intelex solution characteristics identified from the configuration include:

- **Workflow architecture:** Multiple incident-specific workflow objects exist rather than one generic all-purpose incident form. Workflow draft schemas exist for Master Incident, Injury/Illness, Near Miss, Environmental Incident, Property Damage, Vehicle Incident, and Notice of Violations.
- **Lifecycle behavior:** Configured templates and handlers indicate lifecycle events such as submitted, verified, investigated, returned for investigation, closed, cancelled, and reopened. Examples include configured templates for Vehicle Incident submission, verification, return for investigation, closure, cancellation, and reopening.
- **Assignment logic:** The configuration includes Person Responsible / Due Date helper behavior, investigation due date validation handlers, and workflow action handlers to set or clear PR and due date fields when records are closed, cancelled, or completed.
- **Validation logic:** Date validations are configured across incident types, including incident date not in the future, reported date after incident date, investigation start date after incident date, investigation end date after investigation start date, and approval date after investigation end date.
- **Child/supporting records:** The configuration includes Witness, Incident Risk Assessment, Initial Treatment, Involved Vehicle, Materials, OSHA, RIDDOR, WCB Claims, and other regulatory/supporting objects.
- **UI behavior:** Configurable detail, inventory, closed, disabled, previous, and mobile views exist across incident objects.
- **Notifications:** Notification templates exist for incident submission, verification, closure, cancellation, reopening, investigation, overdue incidents, and return for investigation.
- **Reporting support:** Configured incident rate, OSHA summary, location hours/headcount, safety leading indicators, and safety lagging indicators indicate support for lagging/leading indicator reporting and regulatory metrics.
- **Regulatory logic:** OSHA, RIDDOR, WCB, Canada/United States, province/state, establishment type, injury classification, and related regulatory objects are present.

For NDT, the current non-Intelex process differs materially. NDT uses SharePoint, with HSE managers and Niall as the primary users; employees do not log into the module to report incidents. Instead, employees notify HSE, HSE gathers information offline, and the HSE manager enters the incident data into SharePoint. NDT also relies on manual email/Teams escalation for significant incidents, including reportable events and major near misses. Corrective actions exist in a separate SharePoint table and must be manually linked back to the incident, which stakeholders identified as a pain point.

### 7. Assumptions, Risks & Constraints

#### Assumptions

- The uploaded `.ipack` represents the current Integra/SummitEHSQ Intelex EHS Incident Management configuration baseline.
- The transcript represents a requirements workshop focused on incident management gap analysis for bringing NDT into Intelex.
- Existing Intelex incident-specific workflows are intended to remain the starting point rather than being replaced by a single generic NDT-style form.
- NDT’s SharePoint system is the current operational source for NDT incident and action management.
- HSE professionals will continue to own formal classification, investigation, and closure governance.
- Broader user incident creation is under consideration but not yet a final decision.

#### Risks

- **Security risk:** Expanding incident creation beyond HSE may expose sensitive medical/personal data unless limited-user permissions and post-submission visibility are configured correctly.
- **Adoption risk:** If the initial Intelex form is too complex, employees or supervisors may bypass the system and continue informal reporting.
- **Legal defensibility risk:** If witness/injured-person statements remain optional or unmanaged, the business may continue to face gaps in insurance/legal scenarios.
- **Reporting risk:** NDT’s current metrics may not map cleanly to Intelex fields without a formal reporting crosswalk.
- **Governance risk:** Major/minor near miss, TRIR-impacting classification, and reportability rules must be harmonized or escalation and reporting may become inconsistent.
- **Workflow risk:** Hybrid intake models can become overly complex if business rules vary too much by region, incident type, or submitter role.
- **Migration risk:** Open SharePoint actions and incidents may lose traceability if migrated without clear incident/action relationship mapping.

#### Constraints

- The transcript does not provide the complete NDT classification list, escalation matrix, or regulatory obligations by region.
- The `.ipack` confirms configured objects, views, validations, handlers, and templates, but final runtime behavior should be validated in the Intelex test environment.
- The future-state process is not fully finalized, particularly around who submits initial incidents and how witness statements should be captured.
- Any customization recommendation is conditional; configuration should be exhausted first.

#### Inferred Analysis

- Because Intelex already contains workflow-enabled incident-specific objects and supporting child objects, the likely best-fit approach is to adapt the existing Intelex workflow rather than recreate NDT’s SharePoint structure.
- The strongest implementation gaps are not basic field creation; they are process harmonization, security, classification governance, escalation automation, witness statement control, and action governance.
- The current Intelex configuration appears capable of supporting most identified needs through configuration, but witness statement closure validation and dynamic escalation rules may require deeper technical assessment.
- The project should treat NDT’s SharePoint process as an operating model input, not as a system design to replicate exactly.
